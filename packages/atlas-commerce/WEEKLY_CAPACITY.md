## Durable behavior

The existing CommercePayment insert reserves the immutable quote's card count in the same transaction before provider dispatch. Both MAIL_IN and KIOSK/DEALER_DROP_OFF lock the SAME shared week row before reading occupancy or creating a reservation. Admission requires READ COMMITTED: a stale REPEATABLE READ snapshot is refused, since a row lock alone cannot refresh it. There is no mutable remaining counter and drafts/quotes allocate nothing.

HELD and CONSUMED count against the total; RELEASED does not. DISPATCHED, UNKNOWN, AWAITING_PAYMENT and PROCESSING retain their hold. Only provider-established CANCELED releases it, once. PAID changes HELD to CONSUMED in the existing order transaction, and rollback restores the previous state. Browser abandonment and quote expiry do not free a potentially payable attempt. Existing prior-week reservations still resolve against their original week without consuming or releasing this week's capacity.

Each materialized shared week is immutable and takes precedence over the recurring policy. Its initial allowance cannot be below existing non-released reservations. The old per-route configuration/week rows remain historical evidence and foreign-key targets; they do not create separate allowances.

`WeeklyCapacitySharedPolicy` holds immutable, owner-only policy entries with `effectiveWeekStartsAt`, nullable `quotaCards`, `authorizationReference` and `createdAt`. The effective timestamp must be a canonical current or future week start. The newest entry effective for a week continues indefinitely; a NULL successor explicitly leaves future unmaterialized weeks unconfigured. A missing policy and missing concrete week return NOT_CONFIGURED with NULL counts. No defaults are guessed.

The public snapshot first uses an existing concrete week, then the applicable policy, and never writes. At the first payment of a new week, the same payment transaction materializes the applicable quota and locks that single shared week before counting reservations. Concurrent first payments across both routes share this row and cannot each allocate a separate allowance. Policy changes never rewrite concrete weeks, historical holds, paid orders or canceled attempts.
