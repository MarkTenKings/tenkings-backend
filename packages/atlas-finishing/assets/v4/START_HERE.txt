WHY THE PRINT NEEDS BOTH ARTWORK AND PROCESS CHECKS
The previous design had foil strokes about 0.035–0.06 mm wide. V4 widens the
important details. More source pixels alone cannot compensate for ink closing
those tiny gaps. Several fingerprint contours were dark gray, not foil; V4
explicitly makes six contours transparent so the foil shows through.

The supplied print photos also show pale edging around foil letters and
roughness in the black. Possible contributors include white-layer registration,
ink spread, surface texture/adhesion, and print height or craft settings.
Photos alone cannot determine the cause. A nozzle fault is not established.

WHITE HALO / SMALL WHITE TEXT
eufyMake documents a default white-underbase choke of 0.2 mm. Increasing it
can reduce exposed white edging; however, shrinking the white layer can also
remove support from fine text. Its guidance suggests considering 0 for very
fine white text or lines. Do not blindly increase the global value to fix
one problem and assume the small white text will survive. The current settings
and the test strip are needed to select a value for this material.

OPTIONAL INK-SEPARATION TEST
If a white underbase behind the entire black face is causing pale fringes,
the Separated_Inks folders provide another test path using the SAME design:

  CMYK_ONLY: black face and gray detail; choose CMYK without a white underbase.
  WHITE_TEXT_ONLY: just the small card-detail text; choose white-only printing.

Align both full canvases to the same size and X/Y origin. White text is an
overprint on the black face, not a white base under the foil-reveal artwork.
Verify the layer preview and ink order. If printing separate jobs, keep the
blank fixed between them. Do not stretch a tightly cropped text bounding box
to the full label size: the intended canvas is the complete label rectangle.
This route needs a sample test for white opacity and adhesion. It is optional;
do not load the combined PRINT and the separated plates on top of each other.

NAME LAYOUT RULE
Use measured glyph width, not a character-count limit. The name box is
28.5 mm wide, with 7.65 mm available for one or two lines. Target 3.6 mm
ink height. Keep a name on one line if it fits; otherwise break at a space
into two balanced lines and uniformly reduce both lines only as necessary.
Never stretch horizontally, silently truncate a name, or split an unbroken
word. Use a 2.8 mm minimum. Extremely long surnames such as ANTETOKOUNMPO
need an explicit alternate condensed layout and are flagged for review;
this revision does not silently change the approved headline typeface.
NAME_LAYOUT_EXAMPLES.json includes common examples and overflow cases.
