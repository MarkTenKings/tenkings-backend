# Final approved ATLAS design direction

## Vision and working relationship

Mark's vision is Apple-like clarity, imagery and graceful motion, with precision instruments from a century in the future. **Pure white means #ffffff**, never green-tinted white. The card is the centerpiece. The interface must explain the evidence to a first-time customer and make staff decisions obvious without an information overload. Motion should feel smooth and informative, like a great ballet dancer. Precision is earned by real measurement, not invented sci-fi telemetry.

Mark asked for a true separate designer, using a legendary Ive-inspired persona. The original critic independently agreed that individual findings should remain visible. Do not substitute the lead's own opinion and attribute it to a designer. Consult the preserved original; accurately distinguish source review, screenshot review, live visual inspection and inference.

## Customer report: evidence first

- Show **every individual finding by default**. Mark repeatedly rejected area/type groupings and the designer agreed. Final navigation is Whole card → Finding. There is no primary grouping toggle. Do not quietly restore grouping as a density solution.
- Desktop: Front on the left, Back on the right, simultaneously. Front labels outboard left, Back labels outboard right. Keep images large and use white space intentionally. Mobile: one side at a time with clear Front/Back controls and the complete finding index. A clean front can honestly have zero findings.
- Findings appear as their own red trace silhouettes rather than generic white dots. Enlarge overview silhouettes slightly (approved study 1.8×) to be discoverable; retain the true spatial anchor. Detail shows the actual saved trace at exact scale. Do not change stored evidence geometry to improve visibility.
- Both the on-card finding and its label are clickable/keyboard reachable. Each callout has a stable number, short defect name and recognizable silhouette. Compact leaders stay continuous; emphasize the active connection. No long disconnected lines across an otherwise empty canvas. Labels remain independent even when saved category membership overlaps.
- Detail pairs a marked view with an unmarked view of the same crop, zoom and photograph. Precision dimensions appear there. Finding-to-finding motion pulls back to establish card location and approaches the next saved finding before instruments reveal. Return restores the full overview and remembers selection.
- Centering shows physical edge, printed border and center lines distinctly. Four calibrated millimeter pills sit beside their actual border rulers, matching Mark's supplied reference; they do not live only in a summary below the card. Hover/focus/click emphasizes the measurement.
- Categories include **Centering, Corners, Edges, Surface**. Membership comes from saved report evidence. A finding can belong to more than one category; counts need not sum to total independent findings.

## The ATLAS fingerprint — Mark's brand concept

Mark originated the idea: expand each card's recorded defect shapes, physical sizes and positions into a consistent, visually distinctive fingerprint, entering and retreating like an ocean tide. This is central brand expression, not a disposable decoration.

- Gold is the ATLAS logo color **#cda955**. Red defect origins remain anchored and visible. **Keep the card photograph visible underneath** the gold contours; the later explicit approval supersedes early proposals that faded the photograph completely into white.
- Visible geometry grows from the real approved evidence through a fixed deterministic contour recipe. Same approved inputs reproduce the same pattern. Shape, size and position directly affect it. Do not randomize whorls, use identity hashes to invent unrelated shapes, force defects into an A/thumb silhouette, or synthesize defects on clean sides.
- Front and Back retain their own orientation and locations. The gold contours are a visualization, not measured surface topography or additional damage.
- Provide the ATLAS logo with the discoverable text **Card fingerprint**, with easy return to photographs/findings. Tide flows out from the fixed traces and back toward them; finished inspection rests still. Empty sides honestly state no recorded defects.
- Mark speculated about 99.9% physical uniqueness; that is not a validated claim. Production does not claim this artwork authenticates or uniquely recognizes a physical card. Preserve the beauty and deterministic evidence binding without presenting unsupported probabilities.

## Motion language

Finite arrival, then still inspection. Motion reveals relationships and never makes a precise target hard to select. No endless spinning card, decorative bobbing, repeated pulsing alarms or fake scan results. Desktop retains both sides rather than hiding evidence behind an automatic flip. Optional inspection tour moves through saved centering/corners/edges/surface evidence; ordinary manual interaction cancels it. Reduced-motion preferences and printing show clear static outcomes. Camera transitions, instruments and fingerprint use coherent timing; reveal measurements once location is established.

## “A grade you can follow”

Mark approved **Measurement → calculation → grade**, with a precise, mathematical dashboard. Show category score, weight and deduction, saved side blending and saved rounding policy so customers can follow the actual calculation. Category links navigate the relevant evidence. Do not infer unsupported per-defect additive penalties.

Approved sequence: Centering row → Corners → Edges → Surface → calculation sections → a card thumbnail traveling along a horizontal score axis, decelerating to the raw result → a deliberate raw-score pause → a separate rounding movement (lift/settle as appropriate) → final grade reveal. Replay, pause/resume and immediate final result are supported. Upward, downward and unchanged awards must all be honest.

The approved mockup showed **9.35 → 9.5 as an explicitly labeled design example**, not Abomasnow's grade. Live saved examples are Abomasnow **9.85 → 10**, Maye **9.662651939031448 → 9.5**, Dart **9.978571428571435 → 10**. Existing historical tenth-point awards retain their saved policy. Current report story uses 70/30 side weights and 25% category weights from verified explanation; do not change grading policy to match visual design. Additive category deductions and non-additive individual finding marginal effects must remain distinct.

## Staff rapid review

Mark approved the overall simplified flow, but rejected a tiny card below a large warning banner. Card-first layout gives the photograph most of the viewport, with a slim inspector (~280px desktop), stable task box and precision loupe. Original observations move into the inspector. Keep the geometry magnifier that appears during adjustment; Mark singled it out as especially useful.

The permanent **Your review** box always says what the human must do on the current screen. Routine case: confirm geometry/findings that ATLAS/ASTRA prepared successfully. Correction case: explicitly identify missing/uncertain geometry or other unresolved work and show the recovery action. Saving, verification, unsaved changes, blockers and errors use clear state text in the same location.

**Owner chose B:** neon-red border AND task heading for routine confirmation as well as corrections. Earlier designer advice reserved red only for corrections; Mark explicitly preferred B, so B is the approved implementation. Do not reverse this settled choice under the banner of semantic-color best practice. Status words distinguish urgency and required action; avoid flashing alerts.

Steps: Front geometry → Back geometry → Findings → Grade. Keep primary action/Back controls stable and large card accessible. Geometry manipulation uses actual saved coordinates, missing-outline placement requires all four corners, keyboard nudges are 1px/Shift10px, and loupe helps exact placement. Findings has a large single image with optional Compare and real Add/Edit/Reject controls. Existing verification, draft, save, navigation and approval gates stay authoritative.

Unmeasurable originals: if false, reject through supported decision. If real, tracing it alone currently cannot resolve the original under the backend contract. Keep it pending and state the limitation. Never force advancement or advise false rejection to make a demo appear complete.
