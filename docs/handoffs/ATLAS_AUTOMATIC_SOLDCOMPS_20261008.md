## Investigation resolution

The [original investigation](ATLAS_SOLDCOMPS_INVESTIGATION_20261008.md) found two independent causes: approval never scheduled a search, and successful broad provider results were filtered into empty previews. It also reproduced Non-Holo and Black & White variant false positives and showed that saved previews were difficult to rediscover. These were not established credential, quota or provider-outage failures.

- **Approval now owns durable work.** One job is atomically bound to the exact human approval, source/report hashes and immutable publication. A leased worker delivers pending publication and searches independently of the browser. Approval replay cannot buy duplicate work; grading completion does not wait for SoldComps. Dedicated market concurrency 2 is a simultaneous resource limit, not a limit of two approved cards.
- **Provider response and local projection are separate.** Normalized provider evidence is retained before filtering. Processing/storage recovery reuses it. Definitive typed refusals use finite backoff. An ambiguous paid dispatch remains UNKNOWN and never blindly repurchases. A late retained response can reconcile locally.
- **Manual recovery is explicit and truthful.** FAILED with retained usable response offers Retry saved results and aliases the new recovery request to the original preview, with immutable audit. A conclusively expired response yields `MARKET_PREVIEW_EXPIRED` and Start fresh search; only an explicit new request creates a new paid-search identity. UNKNOWN offers a free saved-status check. Earlier responses, previews and grading bytes remain unchanged.
- **Saved evidence is automatically rediscovered.** Presentation GET and GET `/presentation/market` expose the latest approval-bound state/candidates without dispatch. Reloads and other currently authorized card sessions can read it. States include NOT_REQUESTED, QUEUED, SEARCHING, READY, FAILED, UNKNOWN and UNAVAILABLE. Approval binding prevents stale adjacent-approval responses from being displayed.
- **Public reference selection remains deliberate.** Selection requires exact preview hash, eligible IDs and current approval/publication authority. Automatic retrieval does not change the awarded grade, findings, images, report hashes, valuation or public references.
- **Legacy recovery has an exact plan.** Server-only inventory distinguishes missing searches, usable prior previews, explicit legacy refreshes, revoked access and unknown outcomes. Applying a reviewed plan queues only its exact eligible approvals/refresh identities; it does not rewrite or silently rerun every old record. Its reviewed production run queued exactly three missing searches and two explicitly reviewed legacy refreshes, and all five reached READY with saved responses.

The old investigation observed 180 returned listings becoming 0 selectable rows across three manual requests. It did not retain the rejected titles, so neither the old report nor this release can truthfully assign an exact share of those exclusions to the parser defect. Current fetched/eligible/exclusion diagnostics and retained response evidence make new results inspectable.

## Identity, price and grade boundaries

The shared SoldComps engine uses opt-in `ATLAS_IDENTITY_V1`; the existing Ten Kings default envelope remains compatible. It distinguishes unknown target variants from explicitly confirmed base, recognizes Non-Holo negation and Black & White series context, and handles observed Pokémon finish/pattern, language, edition and stamp evidence plus sports color/pattern, serial limit, autograph and memorabilia claims. The approved human `parallel` field supplies target facts; help text explains what to record. Machine suggestions are never silently adopted.

Explicit identity conflicts and lots are excluded. Collector-number leading zeroes normalize; a missing Pokémon denominator is incomplete evidence, while a different stated denominator is a conflict. Product/insert anchors and other shared research title checks are reused. This is evidence parsing, not a claim that every marketplace title identifies printing correctly: absent or ambiguous facts stay UNKNOWN for review.

Selectable and public sale rows require supported, disclosed positive USD sold prices. Raw cards can now be represented with their stated condition. Unsupported/undisclosed price evidence is counted honestly; an unknown accepted Best Offer price is never replaced with an offer/listing amount and claimed as the sale. No automatic currency conversion or inferred accepted price is added.

Groups preserve actual grader, numeric grade and designation, plus observed identity labels. PSA10, BGS10BlackLabel, CGC10Pristine, Perfect and other explicit labels are not equated. A plain BGS10/CGC10 with no disclosed designation stays distinct from a confirmed standard label. Raw condition is separate from graded buckets. Unknown language, printing, variant and identity evidence is not silently pooled with a confirmed alternative. There is no ATLAS-to-PSA grade conversion.
