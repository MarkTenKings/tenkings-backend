## Deterministic planning design

1. **Snapshot and explain each target.** Read an authenticated complete inventory snapshot and versioned business rules. A target is a stable location/machine/product/door scope with category, tier, par, capacity, active dates and approved channel. Category and tier are policy attributes of that scope, not deductions from a card's asking price. Validate machine-wide versus door scopes so they cannot count the same stock twice.
2. **Compute supported deficits.** At one displayed cutoff, `estimated stock = latest comparable physical count + later loads + returns − removals − completed sales`. This is an evidence-derived estimate through that cutoff, not a fresh physical count. Require complete, non-overlapping sales/movement coverage; explicit zero coverage matters. An aggregate sales interval straddling the count cannot be proportionally split. Unknown coverage, inconsistent counts, negative stock or absent scope mappings remain unresolved; request a scoped count or evidence instead of treating them as zero. `needed = max(0, par − supported stock − approved inbound outstanding)`, bounded by capacity. Count approved inbound once; remove it as actual loads enter stock. Show count time, sales-through time, uncertainty and incomplete locations alongside proposed locations.
3. **Select exact eligible cards.** Require recorded custody at an approved warehouse, no loaded/uncertain membership, no other active reservation, compatible reviewed category/channel/product policy and usable cost evidence. Filter explicitly excluded sale channels. Unknown channel, cost, value or attributes need review under an explicit policy, not silent eligibility. Separate ready-to-pack cards from work requiring processing. Never reassign existing packed product identity merely to satisfy a target.
4. **Apply named rules in stable order.** Use approved target priority and a documented candidate sort, ending with immutable unit ID as tie-breaker. Check card value bounds, pack recipe and margin constraints, with a bounded runtime and integer money arithmetic. Return partial fulfillment and specific exclusion/shortage reasons. Never silently relax a constraint, infer a business default or describe a simple deterministic selection as globally optimal. Human substitutions rerun the same checks.
5. **Make the proposal reviewable.** Capture plan/revision, actor, cutoff, source watermark, exact selected unit IDs, relevant custody/cost/description/price/reservation versions, rule/recipe versions, computed quantities and reasons. Show each target's requested/proposed/short quantity and every pack's card, acquisition cost, intended pack retail and calculated contribution. Keep estimates visibly distinct from actuals. Proposal generation has no stock reservation, custody or financial side effect.

## Price and margin decisions that must be recorded

The staff field currently means intended sale price for that card. A mystery pack needs a separately approved retail price and composition. Planned channel chooses eligibility and applicable expense rules; it does not establish a sale or let the same card contribute projected proceeds to several channels at once. The older tier constants are $25/$50/$100/$250/$500; those are [legacy code values](/Users/markthomas/tenkings/codex-staff-inventory-release-20260910/frontend/nextjs-app/lib/adminInventory.ts:37), not authorization to use them for every machine. `CardAsset.valuationMinor` has a valuation source in the old domain; there is no verified linkage from it to every workflow unit.

Record card eligibility value/range with source, assessed time, review state and valuation basis if the business uses those bounds. A card's asking price can only serve as that basis when Mark explicitly chooses that policy. Never substitute value or pack price for documented acquisition cost.

Define whether the minimum margin applies per pack or to the approved batch, and whether it is gross margin or contribution after expenses. For a contribution policy, `contribution = pack retail − selected card acquisition costs − approved packaging/slab/bonus/labor costs − approved fees/revenue share/other allowed expenses`. Percentages need a defined base and rounding rule; compare integer cents/basis points without floating-point threshold drift. Preserve each expense's actual-versus-assumption basis. Existing staff expected margin is only `(intended sale price − acquisition cost) / intended sale price` and cannot enforce this fuller policy. Existing workflow packing is **one tracked card per pack**; bonus cards or multi-card packs need a separately agreed conserved composition contract before automatic allocation.

## Approval, picking and physical confirmation

Approval must re-read current facts and atomically claim both exact cards and the remaining target deficit through the source's sole inventory writer. Use the existing [transaction lock and complete idempotent retry pattern](/Users/markthomas/tenkings/codex-staff-inventory-release-20260910/packages/database/src/cardPlatformV2.ts:1842). Two plans must not claim the same card or each fully fill the same target. Bind a reservation to a plan/revision and destination scope; protect it centrally across staff and advanced writers. A conflicting move or edit must invalidate/revalidate the plan or explicitly release its own claim, never silently leave an approved plan apparently valid. Exact approval retries return the original result. Cancellation only releases reservations owned by that revision; a clock expiring must not make physically picked/transit cards silently available.

The current [availability check](/Users/markthomas/tenkings/codex-staff-inventory-release-20260910/packages/database/src/inventoryWorkflowV2State.ts:43) ignores existing reservations, and the [reservation handler](/Users/markthomas/tenkings/codex-staff-inventory-release-20260910/packages/database/src/inventoryWorkflowV2State.ts:210) overwrites destination. Physical `state_event_id` does not cover reservation, description, cost or price changes. This is sufficient for today's editable planned destination, not an exclusive restock claim.

Print per-location lists from the approved immutable revision, grouped by machine/category/tier and warehouse retrieval position, with exact IDs/photos, pack instructions, quantities and scan/check boxes. Reprints preserve identity and do not reserve again. Record actual picking/packing separately; dispatch changes custody to a named transit holder, and later verified arrival/load records exact destination/batch and actual accepted quantities. Missing or substituted cards require explicit amendment; they are never checked off automatically. Reservation and printing do not move stock. Physical movement is not sale recognition.

Any persisted plan/reservation extension requires a separately reviewed source contract and consumer compatibility work. Keep all financial persistence inside `ledger-core/write.ts`; no new cash rows, inferred sold-card records or coverage confirmation follow from a plan. [Financial assessment](/Users/markthomas/Downloads/tk-financial-story/ledger-core/inventory/loading-batch.ts:180) intentionally leaves partial mixed-cost consumption unknown, and the [approved model](/Users/markthomas/Downloads/tk-financial-story/docs/DATA_MODEL.md:204) forbids replacing acquisition cost with price/value/recipes.
