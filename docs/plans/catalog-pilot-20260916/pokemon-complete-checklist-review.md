## Legend and counts

- Black outlined square: **standard set** (81 entries).
- Gold outlined square: **standard set foil** (57 entries).
- Black outlined hexagon: **parallel set** (101 entries).
- Rarity: filled circle = common (37); filled diamond = uncommon (41); filled star = rare (13); star + H = rare Holo (30); star + U = rare Ultra (5); outlined star = rare Holo EX (12).

A row can have a square and hexagon together. These are literal positive marker observations. Missing markers remain unknown outside this source, not exclusion. The printed roster endpoints do not provide collector-number denominators. Exact physical finish, edition, format, channel, other languages and all-card-printing completeness remain unresolved. RC11's filled star is deliberately retained as **rare**, despite its Meloetta-EX name.
