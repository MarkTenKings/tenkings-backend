# Inventory eBay value and staff comp review

Owner request: show the eBay sold-comp value on the main Inventory list, explain the multiple-comp calculation, and let staff correct an incorrect Astra-selected comp. Staff should compare their Inventory card beside each selected listing and confirm or exclude it quickly. This is an addition to the accepted website and completed phone test, not a request to repeat those acceptance steps.

## Behavior

- Individual-card rows get a separate eBay comp value and selected-comp count. Staff expected price, acquisition cost and expected profit retain their existing meanings. Bulk receipts do not borrow one card's value.
- The list reads bounded saved-research summaries after Inventory is available. It does not contact providers, enqueue research or gate intake/save. Unknown, unfinished, stale and failed results have explicit states.
- Open the value to see selected comparisons first, the verified sale amounts and exact addition/division. The value is an equal-weight mean in integer cents, rounded half-up to the nearest cent, excluding shipping. At least two eligible comparisons with different exact image hashes are required. Different images do not prove separate sales, and AI matching can make mistakes.
- Review shows Inventory front/back photos on the left and one original AI-selected comparison on the right. Buttons and horizontal gestures provide the same Confirm/Exclude actions. Confirmation may restore an excluded original selection; it cannot make an uncertain/rejected source price or candidate eligible.
- Each successful decision persists before the UI changes the saved value. Unreviewed original selections remain included, and the UI distinguishes partial review from fully confirmed selections. Fewer than two remaining eligible comparisons yields More evidence needed.
- Preserve the original research result and its hash. Record authenticated reviewer/time/request and exact job/input/result/revision bindings separately. Replay the same command after an uncertain response; stale concurrent decisions require the current review state rather than silent overwrites.
- Exact duplicate listing images receive one contribution, chosen deterministically without using the price. New engine results deduplicate before estimating. Historical duplicate-weighted results stay unchanged and do not display a current estimate until a saved staff decision projects a valid deduplicated selection. Duplicate-image omission is distinct from a staff exclusion.

Full research reads containing a saved staff decision use response version 2. The updated client understands both versions; an already-open legacy client rejects the reviewed response rather than treating the immutable original AI estimate as the corrected value. Summary reads keep their separate version-1 contract. After the first real staff review, a recovery release must retain reviewed-value semantics rather than restore an older application that ignores decisions.
