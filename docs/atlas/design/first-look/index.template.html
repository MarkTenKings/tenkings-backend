WHY WE CHOSE NFC

A connection.
Not just a code.

Anyone can reproduce a printed QR image. Copying an NFC experience takes an additional step: a compatible tag must be programmed and placed in the slab. That added physical step matters.

The real value is what opens next:
the card’s evidence, ready to compare.

THE EXPERIENCEATLAS NFCPRINTED QR
Open the reportBring phone closeAim a camera
The physical linkEmbedded tagVisible printed code
To copy the linkProgram another tagCopy the image
What to checkThe card + its reportThe card + its report

Both can open a report. Neither a static NFC link nor a QR code, on its own, proves the physical card is authentic.
