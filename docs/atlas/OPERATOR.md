## Provider and ledger

The fixed Responses transport uses exact `gpt-6-astra`, pinned supported effort/instructions/tool schemas, `store:false`, one tool at a time, bounded output and disabled truncation. All output items, including opaque encrypted reasoning, are retained for stateless continuation and matched by call ID; opaque content is never interpreted as an explanation. Dedicated ATLAS project/key, fixed endpoint, no redirects and no automatic retries remain required. Official API/pricing facts were checked during the September 8 implementation; revalidate the pinned provider policy before a paid release.

Immutable run/attempt/receipt/step/outbox records bind runtime/policy/evidence and analysis/review heads. Restricted runner grants are checked on every transaction. Fenced leases, one-time dispatch, deadline/action/request limits and shared worker/model reservations prevent accidental concurrent or repeated paid work. Unknown outcomes retain exposure. Provider usage ceilings and actual invoices are distinct evidence; absent usage is never zero cost. The implementation's conservative reservation policy is a cap, not an expected card price or permission to spend.

Receipt persistence retries only the same already-captured result after a settled transient database error: at most three serial writes, 250/500ms backoff and the original15-second total bound. An unresolved timed-out write is not retried. This does not retry the provider POST or extend the30-second shutdown drain.

In explicit `ACCOUNTING_ONLY` mode, numeric policy ceilings remain historical metadata and all reservation, usage, invoice and unknown-liability records remain enforced accounting evidence. Omitted mode retains legacy enforcement; invalid or null mode is rejected. Activation requires the new migration/builds and a separately reviewed canonical bridge policy/hash/revision update; an environment flag alone cannot turn limits off.

Each tool result, next continuation/revision and outbox event commits atomically. Receipts may be retained after expiry/revocation without granting further dispatch. Human work is interlocked with unresolved machine work. Explicit operations recovery may cancel never-dispatched jobs or abandon uncertain work while retaining receipts, actual charges and holds; it cannot requeue the same uncertain evidence silently. Late actual worker instrumentation remains part of provider/worker operational acceptance.
