# ATLAS evaluation and staffing plan

Status: proposed quality program with executable offline fault harness. No paid pilot or promotion threshold is approved by these files. [plan.json](../../packages/atlas-contracts/evaluation/plan.json) contains explicit null approval, spend, cohort, prompt, quota, canary and roster fields.

Run the [offline package](../../packages/atlas-contracts/README.md) under Node 20 with an empty environment. The evaluator runs 1,000 repetitions across eight synthetic failure scenarios: normal completion, failure before/after commit, lease takeover, unknown paid outcome, terminal provider outage, marketplace injection and raw-finding omission. Provider results and transactions are mocked; the file adapter has separate small reopen/failpoint tests. This proves the tested control flow only. Two generated small PNGs and repeated jobs are **zero independent physical accuracy specimens**. The output leaves every visual/market/human metric null and promotion unauthorized.

## Frozen cohort and truth

Propose 150 development specimens, 100 separate calibration specimens and at least 300 locked quality specimens. Group all photos, retakes and crops of a physical specimen; hold out whole card families for generalization claims. Freeze candidate model/effort/prompt/tools, rules/map/worker and approved Memory before the split. Historical completions already used by Memory belong to development/history, not a clean holdout. Validate source-hash, capture, specimen, family and frozen-candidate joins; reliability repetitions are explicitly exempt from source uniqueness and cannot enter the accuracy denominator.

Stratify Sports/Pokémon, modern/vintage, matte/gloss/foil/reverse-holo/textured, light/dark borders, category/severity of damage, lighting/glare/blur/angle and grade band. Report exact-image replay, new photo of same card, new specimen in known family and unseen family separately. Show absent/undersampled intersections and weights rather than averaging them away.

Two trained graders independently label at least 100 locked cards and every severe/disputed case; adjudicate disagreement and uncertainty. Use physical inspection/metrology when two photos cannot establish surface condition or millimeter accuracy. Include valid abstentions for identity, recapture, expert inspection and insufficient comps. Do not force perfect grades from unavailable evidence.

## Recorded metrics and denominators

Every admitted case gets exactly one known terminal result against a frozen candidate manifest, including failed/abstained cases. The executable metric helpers reject duplicate/missing result IDs and source/Memory/split/candidate leakage. Neither a failed call nor difficult card disappears from rates or labor totals.

| Area | Record and compare |
|---|---|
| Identity/labels/reports | Exact field agreement, unknowns/alternatives, wrong variant, cross-card binding, every material claim's evidence reference, draft/issued separation |
| Geometry/measurement | Boundary distance in source/canonical pixels, mask overlap and area/length differences, transform/source validity, calibration/measurement uncertainty |
| Findings | Recall and precision by category/severity/side, missed damage, false additions, retypes, harmful removals and map/Memory suppression; independently inspect no-finding areas |
| Grade | Deterministic subgrade/final agreement, within 0.5, overgrade direction/magnitude, human changes and rule disagreements; never compare a model-invented score |
| Comps | Verified sold status, exact item/variant/grade, price/currency/date evidence, undisclosed Best Offer exclusions, selected relevance, deterministic cents and sufficient-data abstention |
| Market estimates | Separate realized-sale cohort, range coverage and error; no calibrated value/trend/buyback claim before an approved policy and data |
| Review/capture labor | All-card mean and p50/p95, correction/exception time, assignment waits, handling/capture/retakes, label/slab/physical finishing separately |
| Reliability | All admitted jobs, success/abstention/retry/DLQ/unknown counts, duplicate/lost operations, killed-process/lease/outbox recovery, source/approval invariants |
| Cost/latency | Model/GPU/search/storage/rendering, all attempts and token classes, queue/transport/compute, p50/p95, reservations versus actual bills and fixed idle floors |
| Learning | Human-only eligible lessons, complete frozen roster and per-lesson verdicts, lag/eviction, holdout recall/false-veto change; no self-training mid-cohort |

Use specimen/family-clustered intervals for real cohorts. The helper's zero-failure bound applies only to independent trials: 100 independent zero-failure cards still permit about 2.95% failure probability at one-sided 95% confidence; 1,000 about 0.30%. Replaying one fixture 1,000 times supplies no such physical-quality bound. Severe misses must be adjudicated individually; no aggregate pass can hide them.

Provisional thresholds from the accepted assessment (identity 99%, recall lower bound 95%, within-0.5 lower bound 95%, no severe unflagged misses, and review-time improvement) remain proposals pending reference repeatability and Mark's scope/tolerance decisions. There is no automatic metric-to-promotion function.

## Small measured pilot before scale

First prepare an exact 10-card supervised noncustomer manifest plus API image preflight with one selected model/effort, image policy, provider call budget, per-card/batch hard spend reservations, time window, actual account quotas, current signed-worker canary, staff roster and stop/recovery criteria. Unknown API image behavior must be measured with an approved cost envelope; do not fund/run services to discover it without approval. The per-card prompt is reviewed and hashed after Mark supplies it.

Compare low/medium/high effort and overview-only versus overview+crops against frozen identical cases. Actual image input and reasoning usage must replace token assumptions. Continue with 100 shadow cards; then an assisted pilot, locked 300-card quality evaluation and real 1,000-job nonproduction restart/concurrency/outage soak. Only separately accepted evidence supports 25, then 100 unattended drafts and later 250/500/1,000. Trained humans always certify and separately approve learning. No old worker, alternate GPU, previous model or mutable Memory is a silent fallback.

Stop the cohort on unauthorized certification/learning, cross-card evidence, wrong release, source corruption, fabricated material report/sale, severe unflagged false removal or reservation breach. Preserve every artifact and pending cost. Optional comps/presentation failure remains optional; ambiguous grade evidence goes to a human or recapture.

## Practical staffing

At a hypothetical all-card mean of 30 seconds, 1,000 cards require 500 direct review minutes; at 80% productive time, 625 staff minutes (10 hours 25 minutes). Two trained reviewers each need about 5 hours 13 minutes, plus exception coverage. At 60 seconds, that demand doubles. These are arithmetic scenarios, not measured staffing commitments or a reason to rubber-stamp approval.

Capture is additional. At 30–60 seconds per Front/Back pair, 1,000 cards consume 8 hours 20 minutes–16 hours 40 minutes of direct capture/handling before productive-time adjustment. Retakes, labeling/slabbing and physical custody control add labor. One intake operator can work independently of two reviewers only if verified queues and actual time studies support it. The overnight processing clock starts after the required images are verified. Measure randomized assisted/unassisted review quality and all labor before choosing volume, roster or shift commitments.

Rollback pauses new proposal admission and routes pending work to human review. A newly approved prompt or bank applies only to new analysis revisions. Keep certified evidence, learning decisions and all costs intact; do not reverse-write history.
