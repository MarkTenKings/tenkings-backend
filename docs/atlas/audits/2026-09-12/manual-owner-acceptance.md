## Mark: two fresh cards, six steps

Use one standard-size sports card and one standard-size Pokémon card, initially
with visible printed borders and some observable wear. These two specimens are
an acceptance sample, not a new card allowance. Borderless/oversized grading is
not settled by this test.

1. **Photograph and add both cards on iPhone.** Use the native Camera app, then
   **+ Add card** and **Choose original photo** for Front and Back of the same
   physical card. Select the untouched originals. Confirm correct sides and
   orientation; both cards should be saved without the first blocking the next.
2. **Open each saved card on the Mac.** Compare its working/inspection views with
   the physical card and original photo. Zoom and pan across all four corners,
   edges, fine print and visible wear. Record lost detail, clipping, glare or
   misleading color before continuing. Original HDR retention and full-resolution
   SDR derivation are separate evidence; UI zoom and cross-platform pixel equality
   do not prove optical quality or full-HDR rendering. Prepared inspection grids
   retain their existing resolution.
3. **Check details and geometry.** Check automatic suggestions against the card,
   correct actual errors, choose the correct Pokémon layout, and save. Open
   **Review geometry**, keeping Front left/Back right. Exercise an outline
   adjustment, leave it accurately on the real edge/border, **Save outline**, then
   reload. Confirm the saved correction and unchanged other side before
   **Confirm both sides**.
4. **Inspect and correct findings.** Trace actual visible damage, check its type
   and measured result, and correct/remove/restore only as warranted. Save the
   trace and verify it after reload. Mark each side inspected only after reviewing
   it. Use the single **Confirm findings** after both sides are complete.
5. **Review the draft report.** Check identity, findings and the four subgrades.
   The eligible trained human separately selects **Approve final report**;
   reload and verify **Report approved and saved**. Without current certification,
   record BLOCKED and retain the draft. This snapshot does not publish a public
   report, issue a certificate, approve learning or complete physical finishing.
6. **Check one Front replacement.** On one of the two cards, take a fresh Front
   photo of that same card, use **Photos → Replace original photo**, then
   **Use replaced photo pair**. Verify Back geometry/findings/inspection remain,
   the changed Front needs review, and the earlier approval remains historical
   rather than approving the new draft. If step 5 was blocked, record the
   approval-history portion as BLOCKED. Leave the final draft truthful.

If interrupted, reopen the same saved card and use its saved-upload/request
recovery. Record the exact failure instead of creating a duplicate card or
repeating an uncertain paid request. Do not erase the first observation during
