# Customer photo-first redesign — September 24, 2026

Implemented the owner's visual and interaction correction in the customer app. The account entrance now uses a moving decorative slab wall behind a scrim, bold ATLAS typography, and a slab-shaped **GRADE YOUR CARDS** link whose button repeats the requested wording. Existing marketing card images illustrate the wall; they are not represented as new certified records. Motion has a pause control and respects reduced-motion preferences.

The submission is a four-step wizard: service comparison, continuous Front/Back capture, return details, review/payment. Kiosk and mail-in service panels show the approved prices, turnaround and transport differences and real configured location selection. The two real generated posters are supplied by the lead; video sources use its corresponding verified assets. No location, mail-clock rule, carrier quote or payment readiness is invented. Existing customer auth, historical submissions, profile, order tracking, receipt and payment reconciliation remain wired to their scoped adapters.

The shared full-screen camera remains mounted across Front → Back → next Front. Each side commits to account-scoped IndexedDB before the next shutter action. Every completed pair owns stable request/card/pair/upload IDs; adoption into the existing uploader preserves them across lost replies. The new buffer writes one image/pair at a time, retains incomplete Front captures, and uses an exclusive account Web Lock. Queued lock acquisition is abortable, and each effect has its own lifetime so StrictMode cleanup cannot resurrect a stale camera owner. No filename, name, category or identification result gates capture. Background draft creation/upload starts immediately when the intake service is available, including before a first-time profile is entered. Return details follow capture; complete returning profiles skip that step. Automatic card identity is reviewed after capture, with explicit corrections retained.

Upload progress derives from actual verified Front/Back server records, not an empty pending array. Verified local blobs are released from durable storage and the React snapshot. Device-only captures are labeled as such when the service is unavailable; no order, payment or identification result is fabricated. The exact prior paid/UNKNOWN attempt resumes without a second charge. Same-photo pairs remain an explicit, removable unsent refusal.
