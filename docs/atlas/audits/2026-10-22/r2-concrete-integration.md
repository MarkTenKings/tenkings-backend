## Owner decisions encoded

The external `continuation/r2-owner-commercial-decisions.json` preserves the exact owner replies and question identifiers. The later 24-hour direction supersedes the initial seven-day draft.

- Fee: 8% of card price; ordinary processing included; no listing fee, buyer premium or standard withdrawal percentage. Integer-cent HALF_UP rounding is the documented implementation convention.
- Returns: request within 24 elapsed hours of actual delivery, exclusively severe damage or wrong product, with buyer proof and ATLAS staff review. The request deadline is exclusive, so return admission and release eligibility do not overlap.
- ATLAS initially pays approved return shipping; staff allocates final responsibility after review. Approval does not purchase a label, issue a refund or debit a seller.
- Shipping: ShipStation quote plus $3 per order, displayed upfront as **Shipping & handling**. Buyer disclosure identifies the packaging/handling charge; private carrier cost is retained separately. The $3 is revenue before packaging/labor costs, not measured profit.

`createPilotCommercialTerms` and `applyPilotCommercialTerms` require explicit version/environment/approval/terms references and validity dates. They do not invent reviewed records or operational authority. `createPilotShippingPricing` similarly requires explicit validity, tax code and buyer-charge cap. See [commercial contract](../../../../packages/atlas-marketplace/PILOT_COMMERCIAL_TERMS.md) and [shipping accounting](r2-shipping-handling-pricing.md).
