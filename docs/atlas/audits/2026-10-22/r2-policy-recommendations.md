# R2 pilot recommendation packet

These are proposed launch choices for Mark's review, not approved commercial terms. They do not enable runtime configuration. The exact outstanding approvals and physical evidence remain in `r2-launch-decisions.md`.

| Area | Recommended first pilot choice | Reason and unresolved facts |
|---|---|---|
| Inventory and seller | US/USD, fixed-price, ATLAS-held cards only; one approved seller account and genuine title/consignment/custody records per listing | Matches the implemented pilot and keeps physical accountability clear. Fingerprint and actual authority records are still absent; a report or collection entry cannot replace them. Claims/history remain off. |
| Marketplace fee | Evaluate the owner's proposed 8% of item price, ordinary processing included, no listing fee or buyer premium. Round once to the nearest cent (half up). Begin without an automatic high-value discount. | A clear quote makes the seller's ask/fee/net reviewable. This is a proposal, not an approved rate. Actual processor, packing, coverage, loss and support costs must establish a viable minimum sale value and any high-value tier. The engine supports explicit tiers/min/max without choosing them. No profitable low-value bundle is assumed; multi-card bundle checkout is outside this one-card pilot. |
| Shipping | Buyer pays an exact approved domestic shipping quote; show service estimate and coverage terms before reservation. Use tracked covered service with retained carrier acceptance. | Avoids subsidizing unknown shipping costs or mistaking a printed label for dispatch. Carrier/service, destination limits, coverage exclusions, signature threshold and return-label payer need operational decisions and actual prices. |
| Tax | Use a separately qualified marketplace tax adapter and retain its exact calculation with the checkout destination and shipping amount. | The responsible entity, tax treatment and remittance setup must be established independently. Existing grading settings do not prove marketplace tax treatment. No zero tax default or jurisdictional assertion is approved here. |
| Returns and release | Propose a seven-calendar-day inspection/return-request window beginning at evidenced delivery. Hold seller transfer until that window ends and an authorized reviewer confirms no open case or finance hold. | Gives a definite checkpoint for the initial operated pilot. This proposed period needs approval and clear eligible reasons, condition requirements, appeal terms and return-shipping responsibility. Later chargebacks can still create exposure; funding/recovery terms remain required. |
| Cash-out | Standard manual withdrawal to the currently eligible destination first, with provider timing disclosed. Offer instant withdrawal only when its independent policy, exact fee quote, account/destination eligibility and accounting have been qualified. | Standard sandbox withdrawal is already verified. Instant availability is conditional, not a promise. No standard percentage withdrawal charge is proposed; actual provider charges and any instant seller fee must be approved and disclosed. No spending from balance or indefinite holding promise. |
| Refunds and disputes | Authorized case review, exact component allocations and original payment/refund/reversal reconciliation; no automatic replacement payment, bank debit or silent loss write-off. | Existing implementation preserves these boundaries. The business must identify who funds refunds after transfer/payout, who bears unrecovered loss, and who handles evidence deadlines. |
| Operations | Named reviewers, daily account closeout plus bounded scheduled recovery, incident acknowledgement/resolution, independent process monitoring and a rehearsed stop procedure. | Alert resolution must not release money or erase uncertainty. The actual destination, staffing, response cadence, retention and outside-process monitor remain to be selected and qualified. |
| Packaging | Physically trial the reusable insert, two seals, clean label area, resale instructions and replacement-kit option on a genuine eligible card before accepting a shipment. | Code can require retained evidence but cannot prove fit, tamper resistance, cost, coverage or real handling quality. Record the actual trial and reviewer. |

The next owner review should approve or change the fee/return/release proposal together with measured unit economics. Do not treat this packet as a blanket questionnaire or copy its proposed values into production automatically. Engineering can finish independently; coordinated activation still requires named authority, exact policy versions, actual provider/physical evidence and R1's release controls.
