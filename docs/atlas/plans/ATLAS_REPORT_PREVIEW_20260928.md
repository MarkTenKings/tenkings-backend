## Integrated gold fingerprint — September 29 release candidate

The owner approved the separate tide study and requested the exact ATLAS logo gold (`#cda955`, RGB205/169/85). The actual public report now includes an explicit **ATLAS Card fingerprint** action. Opening it reveals contours from the selected side’s saved red traces; **Return to photograph** reverses the tide, and **Play tide** runs one arrival/departure cycle. Whole card, any exact finding and Centering remain directly reachable. There is no forced report-arrival animation or grouping step.

The version is `atlas-mask-tide-pixel-centers-v1`. Valid native mask row spans are sorted and merged into the occupied-pixel union in the fixed1270×1778 card domain. An exact separable Euclidean pixel-center distance transform drives gold contours with a fixed24-pixel period at full reveal. The field is sampled at480×672 for display. Shape, size and location determine the artwork; finding IDs/order, type labels, grade, card name and mask splitting do not. Front and Back are separate domains. Identical recorded occupied pixels produce identical artwork. Empty sides show “No recorded defects on this side”; unavailable traces cannot produce invented artwork. This is a visual signature of recorded evidence, not a demonstrated physical identification or recapture-matching system.

The layer is gated by the existing photograph hash/dimension verification. It never rewrites source photographs, findings, grades or approvals. Native calculation yields in short cancellable batches; animations reuse a raster buffer and honor reduced motion. Side/section changes, printing, unmounting and changed source masks cancel obsolete work. Both underlying verified photographs remain mounted for printing; the decorative layer is omitted.
