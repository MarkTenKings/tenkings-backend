Historical initialization/checkpoints cannot substitute for that fresh run.
3. **Astra review:** exact report/evidence/crop adapters, immutable proposals, bounded provider requests, fenced jobs and receipts, shared worker/model cost reservations and atomic submission to the human queue. Astra cannot approve a report, publish, approve learning, control money or perform physical finishing.
4. **Human correction and approval:** original deterministic finding/trace corrections and explicit proposal decisions; structured identity correction. Only changes preserving original category/layout/map keys may retain the analysis. Other identity changes return a reprocess advisory without saving or starting a job. Corrections preserve raw findings, calculations and notes, reset the review checklist, and retain prior public approvals. Exact trained-human approval publishes an immutable report version and its exact photographs/traces.
5. **Finishing:** immutable version-bound label copies and QR, separate signed ATLAS NFC jobs, exact hosted verification receipts, and separate human assembly/weld confirmations. Each label face is 2.73 × 0.83 inches with the approved NFC reserve. Physical facts are never inferred from software grading or a printed preview.
6. **Trusted learning:** independently authorized fresh reviewers select the exact candidate subset and record an immutable approval or rejection with a reason. Approved decisions remain `APPROVED_PENDING_APPLICATION`. This workflow does not write to the global learning bank; report certification grants no learning authority.
7. **Operations and recovery:** intake, roster/assignment, exact ten-member pilot preparation, cost inspection/invoice reconciliation, cancellation of never-dispatched initialization, and explicit abandonment of uncertain work. Abandonment retains charges, receipts and budget holds and prevents silent same-evidence reenqueuing.
