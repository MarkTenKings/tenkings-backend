The event records selected `unit_ids`, a complete unique `{unit_id,state_event_id}` expected-state roster, sourced `reason`, and one typed correction:

- `processing` records an observed processing stage and product; unprocessed means product null. Packed product correction retains the current packed stage and exact existing pack. It cannot create/unpack a pack or demote a permanent-card-linked unit below processed.
- `packing` supplies exactly one unique pack per selected processed unit, or an empty roster for explicitly unpacked stock. Retired pack IDs stay reserved forever; a current unchanged pack may remain.
- `custody` identifies the actual `hq:` at an existing Location or named `transit:` destination. It does not deliver to a machine or change a custody identity's Location meaning.

Every unit must be explicitly held outside an active machine batch. Receipt/lot/cycle identities, quantity, acquisition cents, permanent-card links and original physical events cannot change. The expected anchors bind the exact current physical state; source replay rejects stale or causally impossible entries and any correction backdated before an already accepted later physical event. Ordinary physical steps and corrections advance the anchor; prices, costs and planned reservations remain separate.

The existing employee page exposes **Correct held stock**. It shows the current selected physical facts and evidence before the operator chooses the corrected fact and reason. Preview and recording use the ordinary locked writer; exact unresolved submissions retain their request and state anchors through retry. Loaded membership is historical and cannot identify which unit is still held. A real identified removal/return is required before correcting such stock; the control does not fabricate movement evidence, individual sales or a different acquisition.
