A CLOSER LOOK

Four parts of every grade.

ATLAS examines the same four elements on the front and back of each card. The saved report connects the result to the evidence.

Compare the printed borders with the card’s edges.

02

03

Edges

Trace wear along the sides of the card.

04

Surface

Look closely for marks across the front and back.

THE ATLAS FINGERPRINT

The details leave a signature.

An approved report preserves where each reviewed finding was recorded. Those saved shapes can also form a repeatable visual fingerprint of that report’s evidence.

A visual record of saved findings. It does not independently authenticate a physical card.

A DIRECT CONNECTION

Tap. Open. Compare.

When a finished ATLAS card has a verified NFC tag, a compatible phone can open its saved report. Compare the physical card with its recorded photographs and findings.

The tap opens a link to the report; it is not a cryptographic authenticity test.
